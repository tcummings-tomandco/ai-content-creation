All sources accessed 2026-09-03 via web search corroborated across two or more independent legal-analysis summaries, since direct fetch of gov.uk / legislation.gov.uk / judiciary.uk was blocked by this session's network egress policy. Every citation below links to the primary source URL, not to a summary site.

1. **Getty Images (US) Inc & Ors v Stability AI Ltd [2025] EWHC 2863 (Ch)** — Courts and Tribunals Judiciary
   https://www.judiciary.uk/judgments/getty-images-v-stability-ai/
   Supports: judgment date (4 November 2025), Getty's abandonment of primary copyright/database right claims, dismissal of the secondary copyright infringement claim, the "AI model weights are not a copy" finding, the narrow trademark finding under ss.10(1)/10(2) TMA 1994, dismissal of the s.10(3) claim, the 16-17 December 2025 consequentials hearing, the costs order (69.4% / £4,395,656.89 interim payment), and Getty's permission to appeal on the secondary infringement point.

   https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/17

   https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/26/section/10

## Layer 2 calculation — methodology note

**Claim:** The government published its statutory Report on Copyright and Artificial Intelligence one day inside its own nine-month statutory deadline.

**Workings:** Royal assent of the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025: 19 June 2025 (source 8). Sections 135 and 136 require the Secretary of State to publish the report and impact assessment "within the period of 9 months beginning with the day on which this Act is passed" (source 9). Nine calendar months from 19 June 2025 is 19 March 2026. The report was published 18 March 2026 (source 7), one calendar day before that statutory deadline.

**Neither source publishes this elapsed/margin figure itself.** Both the royal assent date and the report's publication date are separately and independently dated on legislation.gov.uk and gov.uk; the one-day margin is Tom & Co's own derivation from the two dates, in the same style as prior Layer 2 entries in `data/stat_bank.json` (see stat_022, a comparable calendar-day-gap calculation for the same Act).
