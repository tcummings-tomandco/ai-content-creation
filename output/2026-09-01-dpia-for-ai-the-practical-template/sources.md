# Sources: What does a DPIA for AI need to include under UK GDPR?

Topic ID: 36 | Cluster: Risk, Governance & Legal | Slug: dpia-for-ai-the-practical-template

## Primary sources cited inline

1. **ICO, Examples of processing 'likely to result in high risk'**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/examples-of-processing-likely-to-result-in-high-risk/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: the ICO's published Article 35(4) list adding ten further processing types requiring a DPIA on top of UK GDPR's three statutory categories (thirteen in total), including innovative technology such as AI and machine learning.

2. **ICO, When do we need to do a DPIA?**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/when-do-we-need-to-do-a-dpia/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: the nine European risk criteria the ICO's screening test draws on, and the rule of thumb that meeting two or more criteria signals a DPIA is needed.

3. **ONS, Artificial intelligence in UK businesses**
   URL: https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/business/businessservices/articles/artificialintelligenceinukbusinesses/2023to2026
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: 29% of UK businesses reported using at least one AI technology in June 2026, up 8 percentage points on a year earlier (Business Insights and Conditions Survey).

4. **ICO, How do we do a DPIA?**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/how-do-we-do-a-dpia/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: the ICO's standard seven-step DPIA process, in force since the UK GDPR/DPA 2018 came into effect, covering describing the processing, identifying and assessing risks, and identifying mitigation measures.

5. **ICO, Step 1: Identify the need for a DPIA (children's code DPIA tool, online retail worked example)**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/dpia-tools/online-retail/step-1-identify-the-need-for-a-dpia/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: naming and worked description of Step 1 of the ICO's DPIA process.

6. **ICO, Step 3: Consultation process (children's code DPIA tool, online retail worked example)**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/dpia-tools/online-retail/step-3-consultation-process/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: naming and worked description of the consultation step, including the ICO's own example of user testing and feedback questionnaires.

7. **ICO, Step 4: Assess necessity and proportionality (children's code DPIA tool, online retail worked example)**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/dpia-tools/online-retail/step-4-assess-necessity-and-proportionality/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: naming and worked description of the necessity and proportionality assessment step.

8. **ICO, Step 7: Sign off and record outcomes (children's code DPIA tool, online retail worked example)**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/dpia-tools/online-retail/step-7-sign-off-and-record-outcomes/
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: naming and worked description of the sign-off, board-level approval and DPO-advice-recording step.

9. **ICO, DPIA template (Word document)**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/media2/migrated/2553993/dpia-template.docx
   Accessed: 2026-09-01
   Claim supported: the ICO publishes its own sample DPIA template, free to adapt, as the base document referenced in the article's comparison table section.

10. **ICO, Do we need to consult the ICO?**
    URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/data-protection-impact-assessments-dpias/do-we-need-to-consult-the-ico/
    Accessed: 2026-09-01
    Claim supported: prior consultation is mandatory when high residual risk remains after mitigation; the ICO's standard 8-week response window, extendable by 6 weeks to a maximum of 14 weeks. Also the base data for the Tom & Co 75% Layer 2 calculation (see `stat_bank_update.json`).

11. **ICO, Imgur owner MediaLab fined over children's privacy failures**
    URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/02/imgur-owner-medialab-fined-over-children-s-privacy-failures/
    Accessed: 2026-09-01
    Claim supported: MediaLab.AI, Inc. fined £247,590 on 4 February 2026; among the infringements found was failure to carry out a DPIA to assess and mitigate risks to children's data processed between 2021 and 2025.

12. **ICO, The maximum amount of a fine under UK GDPR and DPA 2018**
    URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/our-information/policies-and-procedures/data-protection-fining-guidance/statutory-background/the-maximum-amount-of-a-fine-under-uk-gdpr-and-dpa-2018/
    Accessed: 2026-09-01
    Claim supported: the lower fine tier (up to £8.7 million or 2% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher) that failure to carry out a required DPIA falls under.
