## Original Tom & Co calculation (Layer 2)

**Cost per tracked AI-visibility prompt ranges from roughly £1.42 to £3.01 a month across three named vendors' entry and growth tiers.**

Workings: each vendor's published monthly price (sources 6, 7, 8) was converted to sterling at approximate July 2026 FX rates ($1 = £0.75, EUR1 = £0.845, consistent with rates used elsewhere in the Tom & Co stat bank) and divided by the number of tracked prompts included in that tier. Otterly Lite: £21.75 / 15 = £1.45. Otterly Standard: £141.75 / 100 = £1.42. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: £74.25 / 25 = £2.97. Peec AI Starter: £75.21 / 25 = £3.01. Peec AI Pro: £168.16 / 100 = £1.68. None of the three vendors publish a per-prompt cost themselves. Full workings also recorded in `stat_bank_update.json`.

Caveat: published third-party list prices change without notice and this is a snapshot at the accessed date; entry-tier prompt allowances in particular varied slightly between the vendors' own pages and third-party review sites during research, so the figures above use each vendor's own pricing page as the source of record.

## Sources considered and not used

Numerous AI-visibility tool review and comparison blogs (Trakkr, Geoptie, WorkDuo, Fokal, That Marketing Buddy, Indexly, AIPeekaboo, Cintra, Am I Cited, and others) surfaced pricing figures for Otterly, Peec AI and Semrush during research, several of them conflicting with each other on exact prompt-tier counts. Where a figure could be cross-checked against the vendor's own pricing page, the vendor's page was used as the citation. A widely repeated but unattributed "Q1 2026, 8,400-prompt brand-tracking study" claiming a 23.4% branded-search lift from AI citations could not be traced to a named, disclosed-methodology source and was left out of the article entirely, per the no-fabrication rule in `stat-sourcing.md`.
