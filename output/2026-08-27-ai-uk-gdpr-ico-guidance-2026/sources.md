# Sources: What does the ICO's guidance mean for AI and UK GDPR in 2026?

Topic ID: 35 | Cluster: Risk, Governance & Legal | Slug: ai-uk-gdpr-ico-guidance-2026

## Primary sources cited inline

1. **legislation.gov.uk, Data (Use and Access) Act 2025** (royal assent 19 June 2025)
   URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/contents/enacted
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: the Act's royal assent date, used as one half of the Tom & Co 231-day Layer 2 calculation.

2. **legislation.gov.uk, Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, Section 80**
   URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/section/80
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: Section 80 replaces UK GDPR Article 22, restructuring the general prohibition on solely automated significant decisions into a permissive, safeguard-led regime; came into force 5 February 2026.

3. **ICO, Guidance on AI and data protection**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: the ICO's core AI guidance, first published 2020, updated 15 March 2023, covering lawfulness, fairness, transparency, accountability, DPIAs and bias mitigation for AI systems specifically.

4. **ICO, AI and data protection risk toolkit**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/ai-and-data-protection-risk-toolkit/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: version 1.0 of the practical risk toolkit published 2022, following alpha (March 2021) and beta (July 2021) versions.

5. **ICO, response to the consultation series on generative AI**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/our-work-on-artificial-intelligence/response-to-the-consultation-series-on-generative-ai/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: the five-part consultation series ran January to September 2024; outcomes report published 12 December 2024; legitimate interests confirmed as the only lawful basis the ICO accepts for training-data scraping.

6. **ICO, consultation on the draft guidance about automated decision-making, including profiling**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/ico-and-stakeholder-consultations/2026/03/ico-consultation-on-the-draft-guidance-about-automated-decision-making-including-profiling/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: draft ADM guidance consultation ran 31 March to 29 May 2026; requirement that human involvement in ADM be "active and genuine", not a token gesture; final guidance due summer 2026, statutory ADM code due 2027.

7. **ICO, the maximum amount of a fine under UK GDPR and DPA 2018**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/our-information/policies-and-procedures/data-protection-fining-guidance/statutory-background/the-maximum-amount-of-a-fine-under-uk-gdpr-and-dpa-2018/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: higher-tier maximum fine of £17.5 million or 4% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher); lower tier of £8.7 million or 2%.

8. **ICO, UK Upper Tribunal hands down judgment on Clearview AI Inc** (press release/news, October 2025)
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2025/10/uk-upper-tribunal-hands-down-judgment-on-clearview-ai-inc/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: Clearview AI Inc was originally fined £7,552,800 in May 2022 for scraping UK residents' facial images without a lawful basis; the First-tier Tribunal overturned the fine in October 2023 on jurisdictional grounds; the Upper Tribunal allowed the ICO's appeal on 6 October 2025 on the question of UK data protection law's extraterritorial reach and remitted the case to the First-tier Tribunal.

9. **ICO, AI and biometrics strategy ("Preventing harm, promoting trust")**
   URL: https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/our-information/our-strategies-and-plans/artificial-intelligence-and-biometrics-strategy/
   Accessed: 2026-08-27
   Claim supported: the ICO is directly engaging with 11 major AI foundation model developers on data protection compliance; strategy priority areas include recruitment ADM, police facial recognition and foundation models; strategy updated March 2026.
